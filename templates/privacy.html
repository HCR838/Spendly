Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Spendly is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and approximate session duration, collected to improve the service.
  • Device information — browser type and operating system, used for compatibility and security purposes.

We do not collect payment card details, bank credentials, or any financial account access information.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used strictly to operate and improve Spendly:

  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To store and display your expense records accurately.
  • To generate summaries and category breakdowns within the app.
  • To diagnose errors and improve app performance.
  • To send transactional emails such as password reset links.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the service to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We apply industry-standard safeguards including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls that limit who within our team can view user data.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your expense records and personal information will be permanently removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use a limited number of third-party services to operate the platform. These include:

  • Hosting providers — to run the application and store data securely.
  • Email delivery services — to send account-related notifications.
  • Analytics tools — to understand aggregate usage patterns. Analytics data is anonymised and cannot be linked back to individual users.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can update your account information from your profile settings at any time. To request full deletion of your account and associated data, contact us through the support section of the app.

If you are located in a jurisdiction with applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR), you may also have the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

6. Cookies

Spendly uses session cookies solely to keep you logged in during a browsing session. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but doing so will prevent you from staying signed in.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service evolves. We will notify you of material changes by posting a notice in the app or sending an email. Continued use of Spendly after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the support section of your account. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
